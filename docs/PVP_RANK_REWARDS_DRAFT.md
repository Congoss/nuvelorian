Season standings: #1 gold, #2 silver, #3 bronze **Arena Champion** cosmetic pet skin. Appearance only, no extra combat bonuses. The season's dates and cosmetic assets still need definition before activating seasonal rewards.

Each rank I–X grants once per player, with durable receipts, whether the qualifying duel was against a player or fallback AI. Losing/recovering a rank, reloading or repeating the claim cannot grant again. The resource boxes above are new proposed fixed-content PvP rewards, not existing random boss boxes. Rank cosmetics are new proposed assets, not existing unlocked frames. No PL4 stakes or token payouts.
