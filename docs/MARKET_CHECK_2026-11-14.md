# Market check — 2026-11-14

The current mainnet market does **not** use the repository's custom escrow contract. `mainnetPayment()` in `server/services/market-mainnet-rpc.js` builds `ft_transfer` calls on **pl4.nearlytrade.near**, paying the seller and **nuvelorian.near** (5% fee, rounded up to a PL4 hundredth). These are direct token transfers. The token contract is not the custom market escrow.

Game-item custody and reservations are held by the game server's `market_mainnet_pilot` book. Listing removes the item from inventory. Reservation locks a lot for one buyer. Confirmation validates a final successful transaction, signer, token receiver, exact function-call arguments, amounts, memo and NEP-141 transfer events. A PostgreSQL transaction locks the book and player row, records the receipt and transfers the inventory item. Retries use durable request and transaction receipts. If the buyer already owns that unique item, delivery becomes a separately claimable pending item.

The pilot remains restricted by the configured allow-list, verified wallet, token storage registration, buyer balance, budget cap, and 100-order lifetime limit. A reserved awaiting-payment order has no general buyer cancellation/expiry in `marketBookCommand`; an abandoned order can keep its lot reserved and block that buyer's next order. This is an existing pilot limitation to resolve before broadening trading access. Do not simply expire an order without reconciling any on-chain payment first.

`contracts/market-escrow` is a separate implementation. Its intended flow is `ft_transfer_call` → operator `release` or `refund`; buyer timeout refunds are implemented. The active payment builder and confirmer do not call it. Its README and the hackathon checklist explicitly mark live integration as unfinished. No on-chain deployment/account state was verified in this check; no funds moved, wallets signed, or contracts deployed.

Validation: **95 market tests** and **8 built-Wasm escrow tests** passed. Coverage includes item custody, exact fees, idempotency, failed/forged payments, conflicting reservations, wallet/access checks, agents, settlement and contract refund/payout permissions. Contract tests use an in-memory NEAR runtime, not live testnet. Local API/browser checks of online PvP are separate from these market tests. A real-chain end-to-end purchase was not performed.
